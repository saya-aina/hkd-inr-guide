ISRO tests cryogenic engine for next GSLV navigation launch

ISRO completed a flight acceptance test of the third-stage cryogenic engine for the GSLV-F19 rocket on 5 November at its Mahendragiri propulsion complex in Tamil Nadu. The rocket is linked to the NVS-04 navigation satellite, following the NVS-03 mission that helped make the NavIC constellation operational.

Young Indian hockey side to play three Tests in New Zealand

Hockey India named a 20-member squad led by defender Sanjay for a three-match series in Auckland on 19, 21 and 22 November. The tour marks 100 years of sporting ties with New Zealand and blends emerging under-21 players with the India A group.

Indian startup funding drops to about $31 Mn in a week

Entrackr counted 12 deals between 9 and 14 November, worth roughly $31.3 Mn, a steep fall from $258.1 Mn the week before. Greenjoules led with a Rs 150 crore Series B co-led by Amazon-backed funds, followed by Olyv with Rs 57.62 crore.
