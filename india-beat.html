Foreign investors pour over ₹1 lakh crore into Indian equities in November

Foreign investors have bought about ₹1.02 lakh crore of Indian shares so far this month, reversing six straight months of selling. The Sensex closed at 83,022 and the Nifty at 25,514 on 19 November, and the rupee touched a seven-month high. For HK remitters the flip side: a stronger rupee means each HK$ converts into slightly fewer rupees than at October’s lows.

Press Note dated 15 Mar 2026 · DPIIT

India eases FDI rules for minority stakes with land-border-country ownership

Press Note 2 (2026 Series) revises the 2020 “Press Note 3” curbs. Investors whose land-border-country beneficial ownership is non-controlling and up to 10% can now use the automatic route, with reporting to DPIIT. It does not change anything for ordinary NRI remittances, but may matter for Hong Kong-based funds or companies with mainland Chinese owners.
