ISRO chief thanks Russia at Gagarin statue unveiling in Chennai

Chairman V Narayanan unveiled a Yuri Gagarin statue at the Periyar Science and Technology Centre on 5 November, listing Soviet help for the 1975 Aryabhata satellite and Rakesh Sharma's 1984 Soyuz flight. He added that Russia continues to back the Gaganyaan crewed mission.

Tanvi Patri, 15, wins India's first Youth Olympics women's badminton silver

The shuttler from Odisha lost her Dakar final 15-21, 21-13, 26-24 to China's Zhang Yi Xin after leading 19-10 in the decider. She is the first Indian woman to earn a singles medal in badminton at the Youth Olympic Games.

Indian startups raise $255 Mn over the week, led by GIVA and EloElo

Twenty deals closed during 2–6 November, down from 32 the week before, but the total was higher. Jewellery D2C brand GIVA raised $103 Mn and OTT firm EloElo pulled in an $80 Mn Series C from NBCUniversal and Hornbill Capital.
